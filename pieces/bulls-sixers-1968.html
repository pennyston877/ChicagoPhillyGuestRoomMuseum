About this piece

This is a program from just the second year of the Chicago Bulls' existence — the franchise was founded in 1966 — making it an incredibly early piece of Bulls history. At this point the team was still finding its footing, playing at the iconic Chicago Stadium.

On the other side of the court, the Philadelphia 76ers were coming off one of the greatest seasons in NBA history. The 1966–67 Sixers, led by Wilt Chamberlain, had gone 68–13 and won the championship — widely considered one of the best teams ever assembled. This program captures a meeting between a brand new franchise and a dynasty at its peak.

The colorful photo-collage cover design is a perfect snapshot of late-60s sports graphic design — bold, chaotic, and totally of its era.
