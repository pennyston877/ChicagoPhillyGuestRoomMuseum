The cover stars
Chicago Cubs
Dave Kingman
"Kong" — one of the most feared pure power hitters of the late 1970s. Kingman led the NL in home runs in 1979 with 48, his career best. Known for tape-measure shots and a famously difficult personality, he was box-office entertainment every time he stepped up.
Philadelphia Phillies
Mike Schmidt
Three-time NL MVP and Hall of Famer. Widely considered the greatest third baseman in baseball history. In 1979 Schmidt hit 45 home runs and was already establishing himself as the defining player of his generation. He and Kingman were locked in an NL home run race all summer.
About this piece

The Sporting News was the bible of baseball — the weekly publication that serious fans read cover to cover for over a century. This August 1979 issue captures one of the great mid-season storylines of that year: the NL home run race between Dave Kingman of the Cubs and Mike Schmidt of the Phillies. Two sluggers, two cities, one cover.

Kingman would finish 1979 with 48 home runs to lead the NL. Schmidt hit 45. The cover illustration — a warm golden-toned portrait of both men with Schmidt batting in the foreground — is a beautiful piece of late-70s sports art. Flip through all 55 pages and you get a complete time capsule of baseball in the summer of 1979: the standings, the stats, the ads, the columnists, all of it.
