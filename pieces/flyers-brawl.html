About this piece

This is one of the crown jewels of the collection. A vintage black and white 8×10 photograph capturing a full 5-on-5 brawl between the Philadelphia Flyers and Chicago Blackhawks — signed directly on the photo by six Flyers players who were there.

The signers are the heart of the "Broad Street Bullies" — the most feared and celebrated team of the 1970s. The Flyers won back-to-back Stanley Cup championships in 1974 and 1975, and their physical, intimidating style of play became the defining identity of Philadelphia sports. When Philly plays Chicago, it was never just a game.

Sticks and gloves scattered across the ice. Referees trying to restore order. Six men who lived this moment put their names on it. Authenticated by PSA, this is the real deal.
