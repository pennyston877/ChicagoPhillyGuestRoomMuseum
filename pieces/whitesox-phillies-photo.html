About this piece

This original Associated Press wire photo captures two of the 1950s' finest pitchers moments before the 1955 MLB All-Star Game — a Phillie and a White Sox man, side by side. The original news caption is printed directly on the front of the photo, a hallmark of authentic AP wire photography from the era.

Wire photos like this were transmitted electronically from the scene to newsrooms across the country, making them the social media of their day. This one went from Milwaukee's County Stadium to printing presses everywhere — and somehow ended up on a wall in Chicago decades later.

Robin Roberts would finish his career with 286 wins and was inducted into the Baseball Hall of Fame in 1976. Billy Pierce won 211 games over 18 seasons and remains one of the most beloved players in White Sox history.
