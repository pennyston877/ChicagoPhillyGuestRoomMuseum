About this piece

The Philadelphia Inquirer Football Classic was an annual preseason tradition that brought marquee NFL matchups to Philadelphia every September. This is the 10th edition of that series — played at Franklin Field, the historic University of Pennsylvania stadium that served as the Eagles' home field.

The 1947 season was a pivotal moment for Philadelphia football. The Eagles were quietly assembling one of the greatest teams in NFL history under coach Greasy Neale. Steve Van Buren was already one of the most feared running backs in the league. One year after this program was printed, the Eagles would win their first NFL Championship. Then they'd win again in 1949. This game was the calm before the storm.

The cover design — a split orange and cream composition with a helmeted player dead center — is a masterpiece of mid-century sports graphic design. Bold, minimal, timeless. It's been on the wall ever since for good reason.
