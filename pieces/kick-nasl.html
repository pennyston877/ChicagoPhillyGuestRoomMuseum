About this piece

The North American Soccer League was one of the most ambitious sporting experiments in American history — an attempt to bring world-class soccer to a country that barely knew the sport existed. "Kick" was the official magazine of the NASL, and its bold, pop-art covers made it one of the most visually striking sports publications of the 1970s.

This edition features a Dunkin' Donuts Night promotion — a very 1970s American touch on a very international sport. The cover's earthy red-brown color palette and graphic illustration style perfectly capture the aesthetic of early-70s soccer in America, before the sport became mainstream.

The Philadelphia Atoms' 1973 championship is one of the most remarkable stories in American soccer history — a brand-new franchise winning it all in year one. This program is a document of that season, a piece of history that almost nobody remembers and almost nobody saved. It's on the wall now.
