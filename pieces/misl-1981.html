Philadelphia
Philadelphia Fever
One of the original MISL franchises, the Fever played at the Spectrum and built a dedicated Philadelphia fanbase during the indoor soccer boom of the late 1970s and early 80s. The Fever were a fixture of Philly sports life in that era.
Chicago
Chicago Horizons
Chicago's entry in the MISL, the Horizons brought indoor soccer to one of America's great sports cities. Like many MISL teams, they had a brief but memorable run before the league's eventual decline in the late 1980s.
About this piece

The Major Indoor Soccer League was one of the great forgotten sports leagues in American history — a fast, chaotic, incredibly entertaining product that packed arenas across the country in the late 1970s and early 80s before fading from memory. The Fever and the Horizons were Philadelphia and Chicago's entries in that experiment.

This program was tucked inside a frame on the wall — the secret stash. A Philadelphia vs Chicago indoor soccer program from 1981, hidden for who knows how long. The collection keeps going deeper the further you look.

Football, basketball, hockey, baseball, soccer, indoor soccer — this collection covers them all. Philadelphia and Chicago found ways to compete in every sport anyone would let them play.
