About this piece

This 1970 Phillies souvenir program features Ernie Banks on the cover — one of baseball's most beloved figures in what would be one of his final seasons. Banks retired after 1971, having spent his entire 19-year career with the Cubs, never playing in a postseason game yet remaining one of the game's most celebrated ambassadors.

The bottom of the program is sponsored by WCAU Channel 12, Philadelphia's CBS affiliate, advertising their "News Plus & Sports — All the Phillies Games!!!" coverage. It's a perfect snapshot of early 70s Philadelphia sports media.

The program also features the logos of every NL team of the era along the sides — Cardinals, Cubs, Expos, Mets, Pirates, Reds, Padres, Giants, Astros, Braves, and Dodgers — a vivid reminder of what the National League looked like before expansion.
