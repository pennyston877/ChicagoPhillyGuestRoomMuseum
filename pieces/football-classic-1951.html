Notable players
Philadelphia
Steve Van Buren
Eagles legend and Hall of Famer. The most dominant running back of his era, Van Buren powered Philadelphia's back-to-back championships in 1948 and 1949. By 1951 he was in his final season.
Chicago
Johnny Lujack
Bears quarterback and 1947 Heisman Trophy winner out of Notre Dame. A dual-threat signal caller who set a single-game NFL passing record in 1949, throwing for 468 yards.

About this piece

The bold green cover with its kicking figure and goalpost graphic is one of the most striking designs in the collection. Pure mid-century sports minimalism — the kind of design that looks just as sharp framed on a wall today as it did on a newsstand in 1951.

The Philadelphia Inquirer Football Classic had by this point become one of the most anticipated events on the NFL preseason calendar. Municipal Stadium — later known as JFK Stadium — was one of the largest venues in the country, capable of holding over 100,000 fans for major events.

By 1951 the Eagles dynasty was winding down, but Van Buren and the Birds were still a force. The Bears, meanwhile, were rebuilding toward what would become one of the league's most storied franchises through the decade ahead.
