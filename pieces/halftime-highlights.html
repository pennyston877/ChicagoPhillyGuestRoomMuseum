About this piece

This is the humble paper insert that came inside your game program on a Sunday in 1989 — the official roster sheet, sponsored by Diet Coke and its memorable tagline "Nothing tackles a mean thirst like Diet Coke. The real cola taste you can drink more of because it's just one calorie." Pure 1980s NFL advertising in two sentences.

What makes it remarkable is what's on it. Look at those Eagles: Randall Cunningham at quarterback, Reggie White anchoring the defense, Cris Carter at receiver, and Keith Jackson at tight end. Four Hall of Famers on one roster sheet. On the Bears side: Neal Anderson, Jay Hilgenberg, and a defense still carrying the DNA of the legendary 1985 squad.

This is the kind of ephemera that got thrown away by the tens of thousands after every game. A paper insert, a Diet Coke ad, a roster nobody thought to keep. Framed and preserved, it's a perfect document of two great franchises at the height of the late-80s NFL.
