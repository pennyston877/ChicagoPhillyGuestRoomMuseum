Michael Jordan alongside Julius "Dr. J" Erving — the passing of the torch moment of 1980s basketball. Two of the most stylistically influential players in the history of the sport, wearing Bulls and Sixers.

The players
Chicago Bulls
Michael Jordan
Six-time NBA champion, five-time MVP, six-time Finals MVP. Widely considered the greatest basketball player of all time. He built his dynasty in Chicago and tormented Philadelphia on the way up.
Philadelphia 76ers
Charles Barkley
11-time All-Star and 1993 MVP. "Sir Charles" was one of Jordan's fiercest contemporaries — a power forward who played bigger than his 6'6" frame and never backed down from anyone, including MJ.
Philadelphia 76ers
Julius Erving — Dr. J
The original high-flyer. Erving invented the above-the-rim game that Jordan later perfected. An 11-time All-Star, 1981 NBA champion, and 1981 MVP — he was the most exciting player in basketball before Michael Jordan arrived.
The Spectrum
Philadelphia, PA
Home of the 76ers from 1967 to 1996. One of the great NBA arenas of the era — loud, intimidating, and the site of some of the greatest Bulls vs Sixers battles of the Jordan years.
About this piece

Two 4×6 photos, one frame, two chapters of the same Chicago vs Philadelphia basketball story. The Dr. J photo captures the era just before Jordan arrived — when Philadelphia ruled the NBA and Chicago was still finding itself. The Barkley photo captures what happened next: Jordan ascending, taking on the toughest competition Philadelphia had to offer, at the Spectrum, on their home floor.

Dr. J to Barkley. The 76ers couldn't stop either. Neither could anyone else for very long. These two photos together tell the whole arc of Bulls vs Sixers basketball from the mid-1980s through the early 1990s — the most entertaining rivalry of that era.
