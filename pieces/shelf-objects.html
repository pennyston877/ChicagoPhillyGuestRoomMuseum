2010 Stanley Cup Final Commemorative Puck — In Glas Co.
NHL
Year 2010 Stanley Cup Final
Series result Blackhawks win, 4–2
Conn Smythe MVP Jonathan Toews
Manufacturer In Glas Co. — Made in Slovakia
Teams Flyers vs Blackhawks
Licensed Officially licensed NHL

An officially licensed commemorative puck from the 2010 Stanley Cup Final — the last time the Philadelphia Flyers and Chicago Blackhawks met for the championship. Chicago won the series 4–2, with Jonathan Toews capturing the Conn Smythe Trophy as playoff MVP. It was the Blackhawks' first Stanley Cup since 1961. For Flyers fans, it stings. For this collection, it's perfect — the rivalry's biggest moment, in your hand.
