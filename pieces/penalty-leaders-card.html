About this piece

This O-Pee-Chee card is a perfect encapsulation of the Broad Street Bullies era — a leaderboard so dominated by Flyers players that Philadelphia occupied two of the top three spots. Dave "The Hammer" Schultz sits at #1 with 472 penalty minutes, a record that still stands over 50 years later. Andre Dupont at #2 adds another Flyer. Chicago's Phil Russell holds down third.

O-Pee-Chee was the Canadian counterpart to Topps, producing hockey cards under license with slight differences in design and player selection. Cards from this era are among the most collectible in the hobby, capturing the NHL at its most physically intense.

The 1974–75 season was the Flyers' second consecutive Stanley Cup championship year. They weren't just winning — they were setting records for how physical a team could be while doing it. This card is proof.
