About this piece

This program is from one of the most significant seasons in Bears history — 1963 was the year Chicago won the NFL Championship, finishing 11-1-2 and beating the New York Giants for the title. This game against the Eagles on October 27th was played right in the middle of that historic run.

The game was played at Wrigley Field — yes, the baseball park. The Bears called Wrigley home from 1921 all the way through 1970, making it one of the longest-running stadium relationships in pro sports history. The iconic ivy walls and cramped bleachers were the backdrop for decades of Bears football.

The "NFL Illustrated" program format with its bold painted cover art is one of the most collectible designs of the era — each game had a unique cover, making them individual pieces of sports art.
