Philadelphia
Philadelphia Stars
One of the USFL's powerhouse franchises. The Stars won the 1984 USFL Championship — this game was played during that title-winning season. They were coached by Jim Mora and were one of the most well-run organizations in the league.
Chicago
Chicago Blitz
The Blitz had a short but memorable run in the USFL, known for an aggressive style and a passionate Chicago fanbase. They played at Soldier Field, bringing a Chicago NFL feel to the upstart spring league.
Hidden inside: Howard Eskin

One of the pages inside this program features a full-page ad for Howard Eskin — "Howard Eskin Tells It Like It Is" — promoting his sports coverage on Eyewitness News Channel 3 in Philadelphia. A quintessential Philadelphia sports media moment tucked inside a USFL program.

The USFL was the most serious challenger the NFL ever faced — a spring league that signed big names, played exciting football, and nearly forced a merger before a catastrophic decision to move to a fall schedule sealed its fate in 1986. The 1984 season, when this program was printed, was the league at its peak. The Stars won it all that year.
