About this piece

A remarkable piece of wartime baseball history — an original, hand-scored 1944 scorecard from a Chicago White Sox vs Philadelphia Athletics game at Comiskey Park. The fact that it was actually scored by an attendee makes it a living document of a game played during World War II, when major league rosters were depleted as players served overseas.

The scorecard is a time capsule of the home front: advertisements reflect the era's wartime culture, with pitches for War Bonds alongside everyday brands keeping American life humming through the conflict.
