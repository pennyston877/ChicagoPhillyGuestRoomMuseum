About this piece

This is the only playoff program in the collection — and it's a Philadelphia win. The 1979 NFC Wild Card game was played on December 23rd at Veterans Stadium, a cold winter afternoon that marked the beginning of one of the most exciting stretches in Eagles history.

The "Pro!" magazine format was the NFL's official game program publication, with its bold, illustrated covers. The 1979 playoffs edition features the iconic two-helmet clash design — a perfect visual metaphor for a postseason collision between two storied franchises.

Chicago came in with Walter Payton having just rushed for over 1,600 yards in the regular season. Philadelphia came in with momentum, a sold-out Veterans Stadium, and a defense that smothered even the great Sweetness. The Eagles won 27–17 and never looked back. They'd reach the Super Bowl that very next season.
