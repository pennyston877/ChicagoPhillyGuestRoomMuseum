Cover story
Philadelphia Flyers
Bernie Parent
The backbone of the Broad Street Bullies dynasty. Parent won back-to-back Conn Smythe Trophies as playoff MVP in 1974 and 1975 — the only player ever to win it in consecutive years. His mask, his butterfly style, and his ice-cold composure made him one of the greatest goalies of his generation. By December 1975 he was defending champion and at the absolute peak of his powers.
☆ The Flyers had just won their second consecutive Stanley Cup in May 1975. This game was played just seven months after that championship.
About this piece

This game lineup sheet is a beautiful collision of two distinctly 1970s things: the Philadelphia Flyers at the height of their dynasty, and Pepsi's "Best on Ice" advertising campaign. The full-page Pepsi ad on the left features an illustrated hockey scene and a can of Pepsi in a baseball glove — bold, colorful, completely of its era.

On the right, the official rosters for both teams are laid out in classic game-night format — Philadelphia Flyers on top, Chicago Black Hawks below, with each player's number, position, and season stats. It's a snapshot of two of the NHL's most storied franchises on a Thursday night in December 1975.

Framed and preserved, this piece captures something that almost never survives: the throwaway paper ephemera of a regular season game that, at the time, nobody thought to save. Somebody did. And here it is.
